Logistic regression + L2 → fake probability

A linear model is the right size for a 121-review corpus: every word and signal gets one learned weight, so predictions stay stable and fully explainable.

Ships with pre-trained weights for instant, deterministic results. Retrain locally any time — nothing leaves your device.

Honest limits: trained on 121 hand-written English reviews (60 fake-style, 61 genuine-style) — it recognizes common fake-review patterns, not truth. A well-written fake can pass, and an enthusiastic genuine review can be flagged. It cannot verify purchases. Use the score as one signal alongside verified-purchase badges and reviewer history.
